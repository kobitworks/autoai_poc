## Visuals and audio

No third-party visual, icon, music, or sound-effect assets are used by GAME-056.

- Board art, regions, selection marks, HUD, overlays and effects are drawn procedurally in Godot.
- UI tones are synthesized at runtime by `AudioStreamWAV`.
- Therefore there is no additional asset attribution or redistribution condition beyond the font license above.
